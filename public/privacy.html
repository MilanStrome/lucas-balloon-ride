Privacy Policy — Lucas' Balloon Ride

Lucas & Friends, by RV AppStudios. Last updated: November 2026.

The microphone

The game uses the microphone only to measure how loud a breath is and what kind of sound it is (its loudness and frequency shape), many times per second, on the device. Audio is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

No data collection

There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no accounts, and no network calls while you play. We collect no personal information. Player names (optional) and progress are saved only on this device, and you can erase them at any time from the grown-ups settings.

Children

The game is designed for young children playing with a parent nearby. Settings, this page inside the app, resetting progress, and other ways to play are behind a parental gate.

Calm Breathing

Calm Breathing is a gentle game. It is not a medical, health, or therapy tool and makes no such claims.
